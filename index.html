Where Do Math and Art Meet?

Math and art are an almost inseparable pair, working together in grand ways, like architecture, or in less visible ways, such as the design of the infrastructure network that connects buildings and society.

Art and math each shape each other.

What Makes Them Such A Dynamic Combo?

Each on its own is a powerfully descriptive language. Together, they spark innovation in both creative and technological endeavors in ways that would have once seemed impossible.
